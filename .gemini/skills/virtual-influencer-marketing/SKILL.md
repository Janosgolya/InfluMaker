## 📱 2. Platform Playbooks & Guidelines

### A. TikTok Marketing (Top-of-Funnel Viral Engine)
* **Objective**: Maximum reach, algorithmic distribution, curious clicks to Bio.
* **Core Formats**:
  - **POV / Immersive Video**: First-person perspective of 18th-century maid life.
  - **The "3-Second Curiosity Hook"**: Text overlay placed within safe margins (upper third), posing a dramatic question or confession.
  - **Voiceover / Trend Sounds**: Soft, intimate ASMR-style or historical storytelling over trending ambient/classical tones.
* **Strict SFW Compliance (Zero Shadowbans)**:
  - *No explicit anatomy, no see-through nipples/genitalia, no sexually explicit wording*.
  - Use playful innuendo, period charm, romantic tension, and innocent curiosity.
* **TikTok CTA Formula**:
  - "Read the rest of my private journal in my bio... ✨"
  - "Madame said I shouldn't write this down... Link in bio for the full memory."

---

### B. Instagram Marketing (Middle-of-Funnel Aesthetic & Storytelling)
* **Objective**: Persona depth, emotional bonding, aesthetic consistency, follower nurturing.
* **Core Formats**:
  - **Single Feed Stills**: Vermeer / Rembrandt lighting, cinematic 18th-century frames.
  - **Carousels (3-5 Slides)**: Multi-angle scene progression (e.g., preparing bath -> entering room -> surprise candlelit moment).
  - **Story / Reels Funnel**: Behind-the-scenes thoughts, confessions, countdowns to Fanvue exclusive drops.
* **Instagram Caption Structure**:
  1. **Hook Line**: Intimate one-liner that stops the scroll.
  2. **Micro-Diary Narrative**: 2-3 paragraphs from character's perspective describing sensations (linen, candle wax, whispers, heart racing).
  3. **Engagement Trigger**: Question prompting comments (e.g., "Would you have looked away, or stayed?").
  4. **Direct Fanvue CTA**: "My full uncensored thoughts and private portrait collection are waiting for you in my bio link 🕯️".
  5. **Niche & Aesthetic Hashtags**: Mix of historical drama (`#18thCentury`, `#PeriodDrama`), aesthetic photography (`#FineArtPhotography`, `#Chiaroscuro`), and character tags (`#BettyRyal`, `#HistoricalRomance`).

---

### C. Fanvue Monetization (Bottom-of-Funnel Profit Engine)
* **Objective**: Convert followers into paying subscribers, tip givers, and high-ticket PPV buyers.
* **Revenue Streams**:
  1. **Subscription ($15–$25/mo)**: Baseline entry fee for daily unreleased content (4x daily posts).
  2. **Pay-Per-View (PPV) Messages ($15–$65+)**:
     - Direct messages containing locked photo sets (5-10 images) or 4K video clips.
     - Teaser copy reveals the intimate backstory and the exact moment things became spicy.
  3. **Locked Feed Posts / Paywalls ($10–$40)**:
     - Unblurred/uncensored spicy sets locked behind a direct unlock fee.
  4. **Tip Menu & Custom Content**:
     - "Buy Betty a glass of spiced wine" ($5)
     - "Unlock Betty's secret attic confessions" ($20)
     - "Private custom scene request" ($75–$150)
* **Copywriting Psychology for Fanvue**:
  - **Voyeuristic Intimacy**: Betty speaks directly to the subscriber as her sole confidant.
  - **Sensual Escalation**: The narrative builds anticipation right up to the paywall threshold ("When the last candle went out, he whispered... [Unlock to see all 8 photos & video]").
  - **Gratitude & Seduction**: Always acknowledge supporters with personalized warmth.
